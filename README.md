# Privacy Policy for Needle Gap

**Effective Date:** July 30, 2026

## Introduction

Thank you for playing **Needle Gap**.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected while using the game and how it is handled.

---

## Information We Collect

Needle Gap does **not** require users to create an account or provide personal information.

The game does **not** collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Contacts
- Photos
- Messages
- Location

---

## Advertising

Needle Gap uses **Google AdMob** to display rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information, including:

- Device identifiers
- Advertising ID
- IP address
- Diagnostic information

This information is collected and processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Learn more:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## How Information Is Used

Information collected by Google AdMob may be used to:

- Display advertisements
- Measure advertising performance
- Prevent fraud and invalid activity
- Improve advertising services

---

## Data Security

Data handled by Google advertising services is encrypted during transmission.

---

## Children's Privacy

Needle Gap is not intended for children under the age of 13.
